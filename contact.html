FAQ

Common Questions

Quick answers before we talk.

What services do you offer?

I focus on AI agents, e-commerce automation, and business workflow automation. The goal is to reduce repetitive tasks, connect applications, and make business processes more efficient.

How much does an automation project cost?

The cost depends on the workflow, integrations, complexity, and required features. Once I understand your requirements, we can discuss the scope and an appropriate budget before work begins.

How long does a project take?

The timeline depends on the complexity of the project. A small workflow and a multi-application automation system require different amounts of work. I'll discuss a realistic timeline after reviewing the requirements.

Can you help automate my Shopify store?

Shopify automation is one of my focus areas. Possible workflows include order notifications, customer communication, sales reporting, and connecting store data to other tools. The final solution depends on your store and requirements.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. Projects can be discussed and coordinated remotely through email, WhatsApp, and online meetings when necessary.

What happens after I send a message?

We'll clarify the problem, discuss your desired outcome, identify the requirements, and agree on the next steps before deciding how to proceed.

Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?

No. You can explain your current process in everyday language. Describe what takes too much time and what you want to happen instead. We can work through the technical requirements together.

Do you provide support after a project is completed?

Support and maintenance arrangements can be discussed according to the project scope. Any ongoing support, monitoring, or improvements should be agreed upon in advance.
