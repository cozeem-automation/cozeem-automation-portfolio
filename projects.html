EXAMPLE WORKFLOW DIRECTIONS

How project work is framed before it is verified.

These examples show the structure used when presenting a workflow idea, prototype, or concept.

01

Problem

Define the operational challenge, business workflow issue, or customer journey friction being addressed.

02

Workflow design

Outline the trigger, routing logic, data flow, and system steps needed to support the process.

03

Tools and status

List the relevant tools and clearly identify whether the workflow is concept, prototype, or verified build.

04

Verification

When real evidence exists, include screenshots, outputs, or implementation details to support the build status.

VERIFIED BUILD 01

AI Product Content Generator

Problem Writing product titles, descriptions, benefits, and SEO keywords manually can take time for e-commerce store owners.

Product Data n8n Grok AI Output

Solution Built an automated workflow that reads pending product records, sends product information to Grok AI, generates structured content, and saves the results in Google Sheets.

Tools n8n, Grok API, Google Sheets.

Output Product titles, descriptions, benefits, and SEO keywords. The original product status is updated after processing.
